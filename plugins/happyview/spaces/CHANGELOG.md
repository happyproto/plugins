## [1.0.0 (2026-10-07)](https://github.com/happyproto/plugins/releases/tag/happyview-spaces-v1.0.0)

- **First release on HappyView v3's plugin API.** Ships `happyview.spaces` as a standalone library plugin: `create`, `accept_invite`, `info` and `query` at the top level, plus a `get(uri)` handle for a space's records, members, invites and settings.
